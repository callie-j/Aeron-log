🌕 Lunar South Pole Landing Site Viability Predictor

A small regression neural network (via ml5.js) that learns to score candidate landing sites from slope, solar illumination, and Earth-comms visibility — the same three factors NASA actually weighs for Artemis III site selection.

About the dataset below: the 7 rows are illustrative values based on publicly known, published factors (approximate slope / solar illumination % / Earth-visibility %) for real Artemis III candidate regions — not exact official per-site figures. Swap these for the official challenge dataset once it's released (Oct 28) before your final submission. The training + prediction pipeline works identically no matter what numbers you plug in — that's the part to get solid now.
